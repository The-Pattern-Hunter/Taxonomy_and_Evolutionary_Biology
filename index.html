About This Book

Every living thing carries two kinds of evidence: the pattern of its body, and the record of how that pattern came to be. Pattern and Descent is an eleven-chapter textbook for the NEP-2020 sixth-semester BSc Zoology syllabus, moving from the logic of classification through the origin and fossil record of life to the evolutionary forces of selection, drift, migration, and mutation that shape descent.

The chapters move from foundational concepts to their application, using worked examples throughout: quantitative methods such as the Hardy–Weinberg equation and chi-square testing, frameworks such as Hamilton's rule for kin selection, and the logic of phylogenetic reconstruction applied across the tree of life, including human evolution. Seventy-two original diagrams, worked examples, common misconceptions, and a nine-practical laboratory companion accompany the text throughout.
